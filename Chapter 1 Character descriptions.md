Here are the detailed character design descriptions, written specifically for an illustrator or concept artist. I have focused on silhouette, color palette, texture, and specific details that convey the narrative weight of each design.

### **1. Mori Midori (The Tragic Guardian)**

**A. Magical Druid Princess Form**
* **Vibe:** Organic, earthy, and slightly worn-out. Not the typical "plastic/sparkly" magical girl. A warrior of nature.
* **Physique:** Petite, athletic but exhausted.
* **Outfit:**
    * **Dress:** Made entirely of living plant matter. The skirt is layered large leaves (like cabbage or lotus) in varying shades of emerald and forest green. The bodice is woven vines and bark, acting as light armor.
    * **Details:** Small flowers blooming on her shoulders, but some are wilting/browning (symbolizing her fading life force).
    * **Legs/Feet:** Wrapped in vine-sandals that go up to her knees.
* **Weaponry:**
    * **Main:** A giant, oversized Green Onion (Leek). It looks fresh and sturdy, almost metallic in its sheen.
    * **Shield:** A rugged piece of thick tree bark with a glowing rune in the center.
* **Expression:** A bubbly, forced smile that doesn't quite reach her tired eyes.

**B. The Nun (True Form)**
* **Vibe:** Ascetic, fragile, pure, and tragically young.
* **Outfit:** A traditional, simple Catholic nun’s habit. Black veil, white wimple, long black tunic. No ornamentation.
* **Physical State:** She is barely 19 or 20. Her skin is pale, almost translucent. In death, she looks peaceful, contrasting the violence of her life.
* **Key Detail:** Her hands are rough and calloused—hands that have worked hard.

### **2. Love (The Mentor)**
* **Species:** Black Bombay Cat.
* **Vibe:** Cynical noir detective trapped in a cute animal's body.
* **Accessories:**
    * A red bowtie (slightly crooked).
    * **ALWAYS** has a lit cigarette. It defies physics; he holds it in his paw or it hangs from his mouth.
* **Expression:** Half-lidded eyes, unimpressed, sardonic. Even when he is worried, he looks annoyed.
* **Texture:** His fur is scruffy, not sleek. He’s an alley cat in a tuxedo.

---

### **3. The Kaiju King (Prologue Villain)**
* **Vibe:** Godzilla meets King Arthur. Noble monster.
* **Scale:** Massive. Buildings barely reach his waist.
* **Design:**
    * **Body:** Classic Kaiju—charcoal grey scales, massive tail, dorsal plates.
    * **The "Crown":** His head has bony protrusions/horns that naturally form the shape of a jagged crown.
    * **The Eyes:** Unlike a beast, his eyes are human-like in emotion—golden, sorrowful, and intelligent.
* **Damage:** He bears old scars from other wars.
* **The Death Wound:** A clean, leaf-shaped hole perfectly in the center of his chest, glowing faintly green before he dissolves.

### **4. The Cassidy Legacy (The Reporters)**

**A. Cassidy Sr. (Young - Prologue)**
* **Era:** Late 90s/Early 2000s broadcast style.
* **Look:** Big, sprayed hair (blonde). Colorful blazer (bright yellow or hot pink). Large, chunky microphone.
* **Expression:** Pure adrenaline and ambition.

**B. Cassidy Sr. (Mature - Present Day)**
* **Look:** Anchorwoman. Hair is shorter, sensible, lacquered into place. Wearing a conservative navy suit.
* **Vibe:** Tired, jaded, professional. She holds a pen that she crushes in anxiety.

**C. Cassidy Jr. (The Daughter)**
* **Look:** Gen Z field reporter.
* **Outfit:** Practical windbreaker with the news station logo, cargo pants, heavy boots.
* **Tech:** Wearing a heavy-duty aviation headset over messy hair.
* **Face:** Looks exactly like her mother did, but with less makeup and more grit.

### **5. Tamami Hanami (The Protagonist)**

**A. Child Tamami (Flashback)**
* **Vibe:** Rural farm girl, hopeful, innocent.
* **Look:** Chubby cheeks, slightly messy dark hair. Wearing worn-out overalls and a t-shirt with a cartoon animal on it.
* **Pose:** Clumsily imitating Midori’s pose, full of genuine spark.

**B. Adult Tamami - Idol Cafe Uniform**
* **Vibe:** Humiliating, cheap, uncomfortable.
* **Outfit:** A plastic, mass-produced cosplay of Midori. The fabric is shiny polyester. It fits poorly—too tight in the shoulders/chest, skirt too short.
* **Details:** She wears excessive stage makeup to hide her age/tiredness.
* **Expression:** A plastered-on smile masking deep dissociation and shame.

**C. Adult Tamami - Teacher Suit (Civilian)**
* **Vibe:** Repressed, invisible, drab.
* **Outfit:** An ill-fitting, boxy brown or beige pant-suit. White blouse buttoned to the top. Sensible low heels.
* **Hair:** Pulled back into a severe, messy bun.
* **Accessories:** A battered briefcase (which becomes her shield).

### **6. Tamami's Magical Forms**

**A. First Transformation: "The Failed Idol"**
* **Theme:** Cliché, Flimsy, "Magical Girl" parody.
* **Colors:** Pastel Baby Blue and White (colors of innocence she feels she lost).
* **Outfit:** Excessive frills, ribbons, and lace. The skirt is embarrassingly short. Music note patterns printed on the fabric.
* **Weapons:**
    * **Shield:** A literal brass Cymbal (drum plate) with a handle.
    * **Sword:** A giant, sparkling microphone stand with a sharpened base.
* **Vibe:** It looks like a costume a child would draw. It offers zero protection.

**B. Awakened Form: "The Shogun" (Yumeno Lyrica)**
* **Theme:** Authority, Discipline, Sound, Vibration.
* **Base:** The "Bandages" from the void wrap her body like a mummy suit beneath the armor (deep purple).
* **Armor:**
    * **Style:** Traditional Japanese Samurai/Shogun armor, but stylized.
    * **Material:** Not metal, but hardened, vibrating soundwaves (translucent light blue energy).
    * **Details:** The shoulder pauldrons resemble giant speakers or drums. The cape is flowing sheet music that dissolves into light.
* **The Mask:** A terrifying "Demon Cat" (Nekomata) half-mask covering the upper face. Glowing blue eyes.
* **Weapons:**
    * **Katana:** Sleek, vibrating blade. The hilt looks like a tuning fork.
    * **Bow:** She combines the Katana with a curved energy arc to form a greatbow.

### **7. Kaminari Torey (The Gyaru)**
* **Vibe:** Loud, abrasive, secretly insecure.
* **Style:** "Gyaru" / Gal fashion.
* **Appearance:** Heavily tanned skin (spray tan), bleached blonde hair with pink streaks.
* **Outfit:** A modified school uniform. Skirt rolled up very short, loose "slouch" socks, cardigan worn off the shoulder.
* **Accessories:**
    * Acrylic nails (long, decorated).
    * Smartphone (always out, adorned with charms).
    * The "Gun": A hyper-realistic pistol lighter.
* **Expression:** A permanent sneer or shit-eating grin that drops into genuine fear when the monsters appear.

---

### **8. Nyall R. Thotep (The Hat Man)**
* **Vibe:** Eldritch Gentleman, Shadow Person, The Devil at the Crossroads.
* **Physique:** Abnormally tall and thin. Limbs slightly too long.
* **Outfit:** An immaculate tuxedo with tails, white gloves, and a tall Top Hat.
* **Color:** He is monochromatic black and white, but emits a faint, nauseating purple aura/static.
* **Face:** Often obscured by shadow. When seen, he has a smile that is too wide and too many teeth. No eyes, just shadow under the brim of the hat.

---

### **9. The Behemoth (Scrap Monster)**
* **Vibe:** Industrial Waste Golem, "The Crushing Weight of Urban Life."
* **Physique:** Top-heavy Gorilla proportions. Massive arms/chest, comically short/stubby legs.
* **Composition:**
    * **Flesh:** Made of compacted car parts. Shattered windshields embedded in "skin," tires for muscle fibers, exhaust pipes for fingers.
    * **Clothing:** Shreds of a white dress shirt and a business tie hanging around his neck (implying he was a salaryman).
* **Head:** No distinct face—just a mangle of headlights and grille that mimics a screaming mouth.

---

### **10. The "Dickheads" (Street Monsters)**

**A. Human Form (The Drunks)**
* **Vibe:** Sleazy, red-faced salarymen or street thugs. Unkempt suits, loosening ties, sweating alcohol.

**B. Monster Form**
* **Vibe:** H.R. Giger body horror meets Freudian Nightmare.
* **Head:** The head is shaped like a glans (smooth, bulbous, vein-y). No eyes, just a vertical slit for a mouth.
* **Neck:** The defining feature.
    * *Passive:* Long, flaccid, swinging loosely like a turkey wattle.
    * *Aggressive:* Becomes rigid, thick, and erect, extending toward the prey.
* **Throat:** Two large, bulbous sacs hang at the base of the neck (testicular imagery).
* **Limbs:** Lanky, grasping claws. They move with a jerky, unnatural gait.
