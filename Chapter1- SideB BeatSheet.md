SIDE B — IMPROVED BEAT SHEET

(Single chapter / episode, internally segmented)

MOVEMENT I — THE BURDEN

Love snaps Hanami back to reality: because of her, Midori cannot fight.

This is not destiny, prophecy, or chosenness. It’s logistics.

Hanami protests: she can’t fight, she’s not special.

Love agrees. Cruelly.

He admits it would be easier to bind with Torey, but Primater already mixed with Hanami.

Hanami mutters she’s barely thirty. No one reacts.

Torey hypes her up, clumsy but sincere. This was Hanami’s childhood dream.

Midori, exhausted, tells Hanami she believes in her.

Hanami swallows and agrees to try.

Purpose: responsibility without romance.

MOVEMENT II — THE OATH

Love orders Hanami to place her hand over Midori’s.

He places his paw on top.

Hanami is made to recite an oath — awkward, humiliating.

She declares she will fight in the name of Love.

Her eyes flash purple.

Her body lifts slightly, unnaturally.

Cut.

MOVEMENT III — THE VOID (FIRST DESCENT)

Hanami finds herself in a vast, cloudy purple void.

Two massive feline eyes gaze at her.

A smooth, feminine voice addresses her.

She is not dead. Not yet.

The question is not can she live — but does she want to.

Before she can answer—

Cut back to reality.

MOVEMENT IV — TRANSFORMATION (HUMILIATION FIRST)

Hanami’s clothes are torn apart by magic.

For a split second she is naked (Torey snaps a selfie).

Her teacher suit reshapes into a light-blue magical girl outfit, music-themed.

Her briefcase becomes a cymbal-shield.

Her microphone becomes a blade.

She lands on one knee.

Immediately reacts with shame at the outfit length.

Torey tells her it suits her.

Midori quietly explains the outfit reflects self-perception and desire.

Hanami grips her shield and sword, grounding herself in memories:

farm labor

baseball

endurance

Love delivers the “crash course”:

Hit it.

Don’t get hit.

Hit it harder.

Hanami begs Torey to help Midori escape.

Love says it’s already too late.

MOVEMENT V — THE MONSTER ARRIVES

Buildings tremble. Car alarms scream and die.

A massive behemoth drops into the open space:

short, stubby legs

body fused with car parts

tires, exhausts, mirrors

destroyed shirt and tie

A man in a top hat sits casually on its shoulder, amused.

He remarks Hanami is a “new doll”.

The behemoth parrots: “Doll. Break.”

The man taps its shoulder and disappears.

The behemoth lands heavily, cracking concrete.

Something small falls from its hand: Midori merchandise, unnoticed.

MOVEMENT VI — FAILURE

Hanami confronts the behemoth, shaking violently.

Love demands she declare her intent.

She refuses, embarrassed.

Love screams.

She finally blurts it out, awkward and sincere:

fighting evil in the name of Love.

Silence.

The behemoth suddenly swings.

Hanami is sent flying, smashing into a wall.

Her sword breaks. Her shield dents.

The behemoth dismisses her as fragile.

Turns toward Midori and Torey.

Love hisses.

MOVEMENT VII — REFUSAL TO STAY DOWN

The behemoth screams — in pain.

Hanami, battered, has stabbed its leg with her broken blade.

She tells it to pay attention to its teacher.

The behemoth kicks her away.

Love and Midori realize she can’t win alone.

Love suggests switching vessels.

Midori refuses.

Instead, Midori asks Torey for help.

Love begins coaching Hanami directly:

Her body is perception.

The pain is real, but meaning is chosen.

Hanami dodges instinctively for the first time.

Love tells her to focus on what she wants to be.

MOVEMENT VIII — MIDORI’S BROADCAST

Midori uses a megaphone, powered by Torey.

A helicopter finds them.

Broadcast resumes.

The city watches.

Midori speaks:

love for the city

love for people

love for even the lost and cruel

A green orb forms in her chest, growing stronger as people believe.

Torey warns her to move.

Midori reassures her with a touch.

She asks the city to focus on that love.

The behemoth raises its fists.

MOVEMENT IX — THE FALSE END

The behemoth strikes.

Hanami leaps onto its arm, dragging it down.

She clings, biting, stabbing, refusing to fall.

The world watches in disbelief.

The behemoth smashes her repeatedly.

Love yells.

Hanami loses consciousness.

The behemoth lifts her, about to kill her.

Midori, smiling to the camera, makes one final plea.

She throws the orb.

MOVEMENT X — THE COCOON

The orb does not hit the monster.

It strikes Hanami.

Purple energy envelops her.

Bandages form a cocoon.

The behemoth tries to bite it.

Its teeth shatter.

The cocoon drops, breaking the ground.

MOVEMENT XI — THE VOID (SECOND DESCENT)

Hanami awakens in the void again.

The feline presence asks if she wishes to live.

Images of her life surround her:

humiliation

loneliness

exhaustion

The presence offers peace.

Torey’s voice breaks through reality, insulting her.

Images glitch.

New images appear:

Midori

music

the farm

her joy

Torey screams her name.

Hanami declares she wants to live — because it’s her life.

The presence accepts her answer.

Light blue energy erupts.

MOVEMENT XII — ASCENSION

The cocoon explodes.

Bandages swirl.

Hanami emerges in shogun-inspired armor, musical motifs intact.

A demon-cat mask descends.

Her eyes glow blue.

Love steps back.

Hanami commands him to stay behind.

MOVEMENT XIII — CODA

The behemoth attacks.

Hanami appears to be struck again and again.

But she is never where she was.

She circles him calmly.

The behemoth panics.

She draws and sheathes her blade almost imperceptibly.

Drums manifest.

Clones appear.

Rhythm tightens.

Sound traps the behemoth.

Hanami speaks calmly:

she is spear and shield

door and wall

key and lock

She draws once more.

Softly:

“Coda.”

Silence.

Rain falls.

The behemoth sits.

Thanks her.

Its head bursts apart.

Body returns to human form.

MOVEMENT XIV — AFTERMATH

Armor fades.

Clones vanish.

Broadcast replay shows almost nothing.

The hatted man watches, confused — then amused.

He disappears in purple smoke.

Hanami collapses.

She crawls to Midori.

Midori smiles peacefully, eyes closed.

Her hand is frozen.

Torey cries.

Love curls into Midori’s lap.

Hanami faints.

End of Side B.
